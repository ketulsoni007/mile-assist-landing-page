## Recitals / Background
Whereas, Miles Assist operates a network of drivers and customers across India and undertakes to bring customers to the Hotel Partner's premises through its platform and referral system;

And whereas, the Hotel Partner agrees to provide room and service facilities to such referred customers under the mutually agreed commercial terms;

Now, therefore, both Parties hereby agree to the following terms and conditions:

## Terms and Conditions
### 1. Customer Stay Bill & Commission
Whenever a customer is referred or brought by a Miles Assist driver, a mutually agreed percentage 10% commission shall be applicable on the total Hotel bill amount generated from such customer.

### 2. Driver Facility
The Hotel shall provide one complimentary (Stay) to the driver who brings the referred customer.

### 3. Payment Terms
The Hotel shall make payment of the applicable commission within (3 days) through Bank Transfer or Any UPI. All bills shall be settled collectively, and payments shall be processed between 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

### 4. Bill & Commission Upload
The Hotel shall upload each customer bill along with the corresponding commission details on the MilesAssist Portal for verification and record purposes.

### 5. Service Standards
The Hotel shall ensure priority service to all MilesAssist customers, maintaining high standards of stay environment, fair pricing, and courteous service at all times.

### 6. Audit & Verification
MilesAssist reserves the right to audit or verify the Hotel's records, bills, and commission details at any time to ensure transparency and compliance. (only MilesAssist Bills)

### 7. Penalty for Delay
In case of delayed payment of commission, a penalty at the rate of ₹100/- per day may be imposed until full payment is made.

### 8. Confidentiality
The Hotel shall maintain strict confidentiality and shall not disclose or share any information, including data of MilesAssist drivers or customers, with any third party without prior written consent.

### 9. Misuse & Termination
If the Hotel breaches any terms of this Agreement (including but not limited to payment delays, failure to upload bills, or poor service standards), MilesAssist reserves the right to suspend or remove the Hotel's listing from its portal and terminate the Agreement with immediate effect.

### 10. Validity and Termination
This Agreement shall remain valid until removed by either Party by giving a (15 days) prior written notice to the other Party.
