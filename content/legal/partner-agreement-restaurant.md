## Recitals / Background
Whereas, Miles Assist operates a network of drivers and customers across India and undertakes to bring customers to the Restaurant Partner's premises through its platform and referral system;

And whereas, the Restaurant Partner agrees to provide food and service facilities to such referred customers under the mutually agreed commercial terms;

Now, therefore, both Parties hereby agree to the following terms and conditions:

## Terms and Conditions
### 1. Customer Food Bill, Commission & Driver Meal Facility
Whenever a customer is referred or brought by a MilesAssist driver, a mutually agreed percentage of 10% commission shall be applicable on the total food bill amount generated from such customer.

### 2. Driver Facility
The Restaurant shall provide one complimentary fixed meal (Lunch or Dinner) to the driver who brings the referred customer.

### 3. Payment Terms
The Restaurant shall make payment of the applicable commission within (3) days through Bank Transfer or any UPI mode. All bills shall be settled collectively, and payments shall be processed between 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

### 4. Bill & Commission Upload
The Restaurant shall upload each customer bill along with the corresponding commission details on the MilesAssist Portal for verification and record purposes.

### 5. Service Standards
The Restaurant shall ensure priority service to all MilesAssist customers, maintaining high standards of food quality, fair pricing, and courteous service at all times.

### 6. Audit & Verification
MilesAssist reserves the right to audit or verify the Restaurant's records, bills, and commission details at any time to ensure transparency and compliance (only MilesAssist-related bills).

### 7. Penalty for Delay
In case of delayed payment of commission, a penalty at the rate of ₹100/- per day may be imposed until full payment is made.

### 8. Confidentiality
The Restaurant shall maintain strict confidentiality and shall not disclose or share any information, including data of MilesAssist drivers or customers, with any third party without prior written consent.

### 9. Misuse & Termination
If the Restaurant breaches any terms of this Agreement (including but not limited to payment delays, failure to upload bills, or poor service standards), MilesAssist reserves the right to suspend or remove the Restaurant's listing from its portal and terminate the Agreement with immediate effect.

### 10. Validity and Termination
This Agreement shall remain valid unless terminated by either Party by giving fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to the other Party.
